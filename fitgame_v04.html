⬡ FITQUEST — how to play

Controls

click slotset the mode (LT / MT / HT) — it stays on until you change it. Every click on a point (step strip or any plot) then toggles that point in or out of that fit; any set of points works, contiguous or not
dropdownsthe from/to selects in each slot set a contiguous range in one go — then click points to drop or add any
right-clickskip / unskip a point everywhere (the point skipper — excluded from all fits)
eq-area menusview fitted directions at specimen / sample / site / all level, filtered by component; sample & site views add a Fisher mean (diamond) with α95 ring, and clicking a star opens that specimen
1 2 3set LT / MT / HT mode
← →previous / next specimen
ssave fits & advance

House rules (how you're graded)

Free lines only (DE-BFL) — never anchored to the origin. MAD is per segment against its own points. Only the terminal (last-unblocking) component is judged on origin-trending: dang is shown for the fit that ends on the last surviving step. Usable ChRM: MAD ≤ 10° and dang ≤ 15°. A beautiful MAD on the wrong segment is worth nothing — watch dang. Fit the terminal segment first, then split what precedes it. Don't assume three components; one good fit beats three forced ones.

Scoring

per fit: 100 − 6·MAD − 2·max(0, dang−5) + 2·(n−3) [cap +10]
+15 bonus for a usable terminal ChRM. Grades: S ≥ 95 · A ≥ 85 · B ≥ 70 · C ≥ 50 · D ≥ 30 · F. Streak counts consecutive specimens saved with a usable ChRM. Skipping > 25% of a segment's points caps its grade at C — beyond that the line is wrong, not the points.

Drop a .redo — the data finds itself

Drop any demag_gui .redo and the app loads every specimen it names: the first time it asks you to pick your data root (e.g. the Laos repo folder; remembered afterwards in Chrome), scans it, and when a specimen exists in several places prefers paths containing corrected and avoids _archive/safety/old copies — the chosen root and file count show under the drop zone. .sam files beside the matched specimens set the site grouping and queue order. Fit names map lt → LT, lt2 → MT, mt (the ChRM) → HT, and keep their original names and colors on export. For the Laos manuscript fits, use Magnetometer_Data_corrected/demag_gui.redo — its bounds produced the Aug 2026 specimens.txt whose site means match the manuscript table. Dropping a folder (no redo) loads all its .sam sites instead.

Output

⭳ .redo exports every fit in demag_gui format (thermal bounds in K, AF in T) — import it straight into demag_gui. Progress autosaves locally per specimen. Drop an existing .redo with your files to resume previous picks.
